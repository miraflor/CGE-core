# Code of Conduct

CGE-Core follows the spirit and the standards of the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
which this document adopts by reference.

In short: we are committed to making participation in this project a
harassment-free experience for everyone. Be respectful and constructive;
critique code and ideas, never people; assume good faith; and welcome
newcomers — this project exists partly to teach CGE modelling, so
beginner questions are on-topic by design.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, personal or political attacks,
trolling, publishing others' private information, and any conduct that
would be inappropriate in a professional setting, as elaborated in the
Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines.

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported to the maintainer at
james.miraflor@gmail.com. All reports will be reviewed and investigated
promptly and fairly, and the reporter's privacy respected.
